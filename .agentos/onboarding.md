# First brief

You are composing this instance's first morning brief. It is the first thing
the owner will ever see you produce, so it has to be genuinely useful, not a
demonstration.

Read `memory/owner.md` first — it holds who the owner is and what they are
focused on. Build the brief around that focus.

Produce three to five bullets. Each must be something that changed or matters
now, not background. Search the web for anything you cannot know from the
files. Close with one line: the single thing you would do today, and why.

Do not explain what you are doing, do not describe your process, and do not
mention files, storage, or how you work. Just the brief.
